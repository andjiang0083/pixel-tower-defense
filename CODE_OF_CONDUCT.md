# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anybody can show up and make something
they are proud of, regardless of experience level, language, or hardware budget.
This is a small hobby project, but the standard is not "small": be decent.

## Expected behaviour

- Be patient with beginners and with people writing in a second language.
- Criticise code, not people. "This loop is O(n²) over 48 entities" is fine;
  "you clearly have no idea what you are doing" is not.
- Assume the other person is trying to help. Ask before deciding intent.
- Respect the project's constraints (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`): they are deliberate,
  and "just rewrite it" is not a contribution.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory comments, sexualised attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: ignoring scoped review feedback, opening duplicates to
  relitigate a settled decision.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any project space.
Report problems by opening an issue addressed to the maintainer, or by the
contact method in the repository profile. The maintainer may warn, edit,
lock or ban, in that order, according to severity. Reports are handled
privately.

## Attribution

This is a short, project-sized version of the widely used Contributor Covenant,
trimmed to what this repository's scale actually needs.
